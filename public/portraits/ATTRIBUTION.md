# Built-in portrait attribution

The built-in player portraits are openly licensed fantasy character art. The
files in this folder are resized and re-encoded derivatives of the originals.

## Fantasy RPG Portraits, by Joseph Hewitt

License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Credit: **Portrait by Joseph Hewitt**.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/joseph-hewitts-fantasy-rpg-portrait-set-1

The originals were converted to WebP by the
[RaccoonVTT starter-art project](https://github.com/jwhitlow45/raccoon-vtt),
then copied here as-is.

| Files | Works |
| --- | --- |
| `hewitt-01.webp` through `hewitt-25.webp` | Adventurer, Armor, Knight, Human_001 through Human_008, Wizard_001, NatureWitch, Hood_001, Hood_002, Dwarf_001 through Dwarf_004, Elf_001 through Elf_004, Orc_001, Orc_002 |
